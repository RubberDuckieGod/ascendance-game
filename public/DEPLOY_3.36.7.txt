PWA / iPhone update:
1. Deploy the four public files above.
2. Open Ascendance once in Safari and refresh.
3. If using the Home Screen PWA, close it completely and reopen it after the Safari refresh.
4. The service worker cache name is ascendance-3.36.7 and includes mobile-3367.css/js.

Expected iPhone behavior:
- Cards opens a full-screen reader, not the old sideways hand drawer.
- One card is centered at a time with full rules text visible.
- Use Previous / Next, swipe left/right, or the card selector to move through the hand.
- Play uses the existing legal card action; Reaction cards remain trigger-based.
- Log opens full-screen above every other mobile control and updates while open.
- Neither view should cause horizontal page scrolling in portrait or landscape.
