Ascendance 3.36.8 — iPhone Explore Placement UX

Fixes the mobile regression introduced when 3.36.7 separated Hero Cards from the old left rail.

Mobile behavior:
- Cards remains the dedicated full-screen Hero-hand reader.
- Explore Location draws now open a dedicated full-screen placement view automatically.
- The Explore view shows Location name, terrain/connectivity information, skipped draws, and the original legal placement choices.
- View Map temporarily closes the placement screen without losing the pending tile.
- A persistent “Place Explore Tile” button remains until placement is completed.
- The iPhone install hint no longer covers the Explore/Attack/Search Action bar.
- Explore is visually emphasized on the mobile Action bar.

No gameplay rules changed.
Custom Hero Creator remains intentionally absent.
