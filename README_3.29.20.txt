Ascendance 3.29.20 — Post-Quest Exploration + Combat HUD

- Completing a Quest no longer immediately closes Explore/Search.
- Quest rewards/status resolve immediately, then the party enters Free Exploration on the same map.
- Continue exploring/searching remaining Locations, or click Proceed to Downtime when ready.
- Existing completed-Quest saves can resume Free Exploration once unless already dismissed.
- Added a video-game-style HUD above the Quest Map showing every party Hero and every living Enemy.
- Hero HUD cards use the existing Hero portraits, current Location, Actions, numeric Health, and animated Health bars.
- Enemy HUD cards show a fantasy portrait glyph, current Location, boss styling, numeric Health, and Health bars that shrink as damage is dealt.
- Build marker: 3.29.20.
- Includes 32 lightweight enemy SVG portrait assets directly in `/assets` (no nested enemy-art folder required).
