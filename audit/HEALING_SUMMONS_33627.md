# Healing choices and Spirit Wolf

The authored card is **H003-016 Shepherd the Faithful** (Elara, 1 Action). It chooses an allied Hero in Elara's starting Location, moves both Heroes to the same connected Location, and lets the acting player choose which moved Hero recovers 1 Health. The previous dispatcher moved the pair but omitted the recovery clause.

**H007-006 Spirit Wolf** (Thorne, 2 Actions) places a token at Thorne's Location and immediately deals 2 direct damage to one Enemy there. The user's explicit lifetime change is **until Defeated**, with no encounter or round expiry and no invented continuing attack. Normal creation of a fresh Quest board may reset old board pieces. The old handler wrote a legacy Wolf field after the one-time authoritative-piece migration had already run, so the final piece renderer erased its old badge without producing a new visible token.

The new shared transition validates the actor, actual hand card, general Actions, legal choices and movement restrictions before changing anything. It commits movement, selected recovery, immediate Wolf damage, card discard and cost together. The Wolf is a saved authoritative piece with an SVG marker and an owner-only defeat bookkeeping control. Compatibility snapshots preserve the canonical Wolf and cannot restore a defeated Wolf. The existing 2-Health legacy representation is retained; this work does not establish an independently authored Wolf stat profile or Enemy targeting policy for summons.

The same dispatcher fixes H003-013 Prayer of Restoration, H003-014 Divine Communion, H003-015 Guided Path and H003-017 Wings of Mercy. It supports both Prayer targets, the Consecrated option riders, Guided Path's recovery/removal and card draw, Wings' destination choice and recovery/removal, and cancellation before any effects. The first Support card also collects the printed Divine Grace recipient separately. Shared recovery applies caps, healing locks, Life stance, Staff of Renewal, Pilgrim's symbol, Reputation and regional penalties, Blight suppression, Consecrated recovery and supported healing passives identically in the browser and server.

Movement entry effects use a narrow shared transition, not a browser-proposed game snapshot. It applies both Heroes' movement tracking, Foggy Meadow choices, supported equipment entry riders, Wild Growth, Vine/Worldroot entry, Spirit Passage, Ancient Growth, Trailblazer and Tempest pressure in canonical state. Only the acting Hero receives an exact destination/round First Visit receipt. The acting client then calls the existing First Visit function once; the existing room-owned Exploration path draws/resolves its Encounter. The ally's entry cannot start another First Visit. Server pending state holds a final-Action movement open until that receipt transfers to the actual Exploration prompt or completes without an Encounter.

The current ca4a216 full hosted run passes all 18 cases in `healing-summons-33627.spec.cjs`, as a subset of its 420 scenarios. The 435 passing Node checks include the dedicated shared-card, passive-choice, lifetime, malformed-input and actual-server cases. Exact current outcomes and source identity are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and [REPORT.md](REPORT.md).

Validation is deliberately scoped. The dedicated Node tests assert the shared card effects, modifiers, atomic refusals, entry passives, receipt ownership, canonical piece persistence and defeat. Browser scenarios cover actual card play, injured chosen recipients, cancellation, visible tokens, save/load, two native clients, and final-Action First Visit with peer reconnect and a single Encounter reward. Current browser outcomes and source identity are recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md) and [REPORT.md](REPORT.md); they are not inferred from the presence of test code.

Optional high-level interactions use narrow canonical choice receipts: Asha's level-9 Spirit Conclave and Thorne's level-7 Verdant Reach first reveal a server-drawn card, then require a UID actually in the beneficiary's current hand to discard. Seraphine's level-11 Sanctified Vanguard validates another Hero in her Location before granting Guarded. Asha's authored level-11 Ancestor's Circle offers one Hero at a triggered Totem either 1 Health or 1 card. The old level-14 entry hook referred to an undefined `triggerTotem`; the authored level-14 row adds harmful-Status immunity, not an invented extra entry effect. The named ability choices, their once-per-Round use, ownership and persistence have dedicated Node oracles. These tests do not independently certify every high-level interaction elsewhere in the game.

Divine Grace uses the authored level thresholds: 1 Health at levels 1–2, 2 Health from level 3. Blessed Presence begins at level 2 and grants Inspired once per Round after Elara restores another Hero. Inspired updates both the shared Condition representation and the legacy flags used by Aldric/Lyra attacks. Fatal Tempest entry records the Hero's fate and creates no unfinishable entry receipt. Compatibility merges preserve consumed round/quest receipts while leaving one-use Armor and other consumable buffs free to clear.

Source findings requiring follow-up scenarios remain outside this focused fix:

- Turret deployment H010-009/H010-020 has the same legacy migration visibility defect. No continuing firing or defeat consumer was found for its stored fields.
- H010-011 Emergency Repair offers Heroes but omits Constructs. H010-019 Master Engineer lacks complete Construct and expiry consumers.
- H014-006 Bone Legion and H014-020 Death Unbound resolve once per occupied Location rather than once per Bone Servant there.
- H007-012 Nature's Blessing has a passing chosen-recipient/Burning-removal scenario. Source review still finds both Bleeding and Burning removed when both are present, instead of the printed choice; that combined-status branch needs a native reproduction and correction.

Other healing-recipient cards needing independent coverage include H003-002/H003-006, H006-001/H006-009 and H010-011. H007-012 and H016-010 now have exact selected-recipient recovery scenarios in the additional card matrix; their remaining timing, status and multiplayer clauses are not certified by those solo cases. Bone Servant and Spirit Totem placement-count checks do not certify summon damage, lifetime, visibility, ownership or multiplayer behavior. Generic full-Health card observations remain observations, not full-effect passes.


Within the new shared card-movement path, Wild Growth's authored Level 1 grant is temporary Regenerating through the end of the beneficiary's next turn. Its metadata distinguishes the current activation from the next one, and cleansing clears that metadata. A pre-existing permanent Regenerating effect remains permanent. Nature's Renewal starts at Thorne Level 2: a living, co-located Thorne increases each Hero's first actual Regenerating recovery that Round from 1 to 2 before caps and Blight suppression. Shared-rule tests check the unlock, positive recovery, no-heal/full-Health controls, extra activations, source absence and stale snapshots. These are not a complete browser certification of every Regenerating modifier.

Wings of Mercy's temporary Consecrated ground expires at the start of Elara's next activation, including an extra turn in the same Round. Shared-rule tests distinguish an actual next activation from repeated/reconnected reads and prevent stale snapshots from restoring expired ground. The test is in `tests/healing-lifetimes-33627-rules.test.cjs`; the ordinary browser Wings case verifies movement, chosen ally recovery and creation of Consecrated ground, not this full expiry matrix.

Malformed choice payloads have explicit refusal tests. A resolving Guided Path is kept out of the discard pile until its immediate draw has finished, so an empty deck cannot cause it to draw itself. The staged `RESOLVE_HEAL_PASSIVE` server test checks that Verdant Reach draws for the beneficiary only, discards the selected real hand UID, charges no additional Action and clears the pending choice. These are separate rule/server assertions, not additional full-card passes.

The legacy ordinary-movement `hero-runtime-parity-3362.onEnter` path still applies Wild Growth without the new temporary-lifetime marker. Its end-of-next-turn expiry is a known source gap outside the shared card path and is not certified by these passing tests.
