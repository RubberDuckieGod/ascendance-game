# Rules completed after the 3.36.21 audit

These additions are proposals for review on the fix branch. They are not deployed. Existing automatic-hit Hero combat remains in force.

| Item | Implemented behavior |
|---|---|
| EQ013 Woodcutter's Axe | +1 Damage when the target's Location or a connected revealed Location contains a terrain/structure feature. Features include explicit `structures` / `terrainFeatures` data and Forest, Woods, Ruins, Fortress, Mountain, Rock, Structure or Obstacle tags. Unconnected Locations do not qualify. |
| EQ025 Skullcrusher | Once per Hero turn after a damaging weapon Attack, roll a Stagger Save for the surviving target: 4–6 succeeds, 1–3 grants Stunned for its next activation. Free Move grants are blocked before the wielder's first Attack of the turn. Paid Moves remain legal. The room rolls the Save in multiplayer. |
| EQ044 Night's Whisper | **User-approved alignment:** the first Attack each encounter gains +2 Attack instead of attack-roll Advantage. A newly lethal weapon Attack immediately ends the current turn and starts a full extra turn, once per encounter: exactly 3 Actions and 2 drawn cards. The room validates the lethal ledger entry; repeating the command cannot grant another turn. |
| EQ055 Repeating Crossbow | Rapid Fire costs 1 Action and makes up to two legal weapon Attacks, each at -1 Damage, against the same or different targets. The magazine permits three Rapid Fire uses, then requires Reload for 1 Action. Ordinary Basic Attacks remain available; the magazine tracks the Rapid Fire mode. |
| EQ068 Worldheart Staff | Choose Flame, Frost, Storm or Life at encounter start / before the first use. Flame adds 2 Fire Damage; Frost makes damaging Cold Attacks apply Slowed through the next activation; Storm chains 1 Lightning Damage to another Enemy in the target Location; Life adds 2 to positive Healing. Worldheart's Gift refreshes one currently used round cooldown (or registered generic cooldown), once per encounter. It does **not** erase once-per-Quest signature limits. |
| EQ103 Lantern of True Paths | One reroll per rolled Travel Encounter. The room offers the actual original and new d6 values, then commits the chosen value, modifier and matching outcome text. Fixed Severity is not rerolled. An unresolved choice survives reconnect and can be resumed. |
| LT012 Traveler's Rations | Rest is a 2-Action, once-per-turn action while not Engaged. It heals 2 Health and clears Bleeding, Shaken and Mana Burn. Rations add 1 Health and are discarded only after a valid Rest. Prepared makes the next Rest free; Regrouped adds 1 Health and draws 1 card. Existing Rest equipment recovery applies. |
| LT026 Lockpick Set | A Location can expose a Locked or Simple Trap restriction through data or authored rule text. Normally Unlock costs 1 Action, followed by Search/Interact. The Set opens it and immediately starts the associated free Search or resolves its defined interaction without spending Actions. The item is preserved if the Search cannot start. Complex traps are not inferred to be simple traps. |
| LT150 Orb of Ages | Once per Scenario attempt (campaign instance + Quest + Day), at the end of an active normal Hero turn, start a full extra turn with 3 Actions and 2 cards. The Orb cannot be used in an extra turn. Ownership, current-turn authority, death, pending prompts and duplicate uses are checked by the room. |
| LT159 Crystal of Creation | The shared crafting material plan can replace **one unit** of one missing Rare or Epic Crafting Material per craft. Eligibility and actual consumption use that exact plan. Other missing materials, Gold, profession, facility, unlock and downtime requirements still apply. The Crystal is retained unless the recipe explicitly requires its consumption. |

The Stagger threshold, Rest baseline, terrain feature interpretation, three-use Rapid Fire magazine, 1-Damage Lightning chain, generic Unlock/Interact actions and cooldown interpretation supply rules that were absent from the executable baseline. Review these balance decisions before merging. EQ044's +2 Attack replacement was explicitly approved in chat.

The original Mordren/summon crashes and repeated Gold-scaling bug are also fixed. Staggered now follows the existing SE033 rule: -1 Attack for the next Attack, then removal, for Enemies and all 16 Heroes. Strongest-Hero Enemy targeting now reads each runtime Hero’s own Attack value rather than Aldric’s fallback. Rapid Fire commits shared combat damage before finishing its Action, and holds a zero-Action turn until both shots finish. Normal Basic Attacks also retain turn authority through final-Action combat, and remove-only combat diffs correctly register lethal Attacks. Incoming room snapshots rebase combat/Hero/resource ledgers without being echoed back as new local changes. Legacy local phase automation and the obsolete snapshot watcher are disabled for remote play; only a server resolution token may advance shared phases. Explicit Action ledgers still synchronize remote gameplay. Root and served `public/` copies are kept in sync. The unavailable historical regression sources were not recovered from Git; `npm test` and `npm run stability:33621` now have clearly identified replacement runners exercising the checked-in tests.

A passing scenario is evidence for its asserted behavior. Exhaustive coverage of every card branch, every level Ability and every campaign remains a separate backlog; the strict coverage gate continues to expose it.
