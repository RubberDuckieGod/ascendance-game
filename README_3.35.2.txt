ASCENDANCE 3.35.2 — ENCOUNTER STATUS + POST-QUEST RULE CORRECTION

- Fixes later regional Encounter effects such as Guarded / Inspired rewards so they resolve automatically instead of being logged and discarded without applying.
- Adds generic simple Encounter resolution for one/all Hero Guarded/Inspired, choose Guarded/Inspired, simple healing, straight Gold, and simple card draw.
- Unknown non-combat Encounter effects are no longer silently marked resolved: the player must explicitly confirm manual resolution or leave the Encounter pending.
- Restores Design Bible post-Quest behavior: after a Quest is completed, normal Enemy Activation / World Phase / End Phase / Death rules continue while the party explores. Quest completion is locked and cannot be undone.
- If all Heroes reach 0 Health during post-Quest exploration, exploration ends and the party proceeds to Downtime with the Quest still Completed; Hero death/replacement rules apply.
- Preserves 3.35.1 Merchant search/filter UI.
