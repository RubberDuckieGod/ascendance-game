Ascendance 3.30.9 — Finite Post-Quest Actions

Fixes post-Quest free exploration granting endless new rounds/Actions.
- Heroes may spend only Actions remaining in the round when the Quest completed.
- When those Actions are exhausted, no Enemy/World/End phase runs and no new round is created.
- No post-Quest card draw or 3-Action refresh occurs.
- Proceed to Downtime remains available.
- Room authority blocks round resolution while post-Quest aftermath or Campaign Downtime is active.
- Preserves 3.30.8 consumable persistence and all 3.30.7 Search fixes.
