ASCENDANCE 3.35.1 — VICTORY EXPLORATION + MERCHANT SEARCH/FILTERS

Fixes:
1. Completed Quest free exploration no longer runs Enemy/Quest World phases when the round advances. Quest rewards stay locked, rounds refresh Actions/draws, and post-victory enemy activation cannot turn a completed Quest into Defeat.
2. Merchant now has live search, Type, Rarity, Family, Affordable-only, Sort, result count, and Clear Filters. Search covers name, ID, family, damage type, hands, and ability text.
3. Preserves 3.35.0 Campaign V/VI, Reputation, generic First Visit enemy spawning, six-campaign map, and server/room authority.

Important: enemies revealed/spawned during Victory Exploration remain on the map and may be fought, but Enemy/Quest World phases are skipped after victory. Select Proceed to Downtime when finished exploring.
