Ascendance 3.34.1 — Quest Narrative Preview UI

Built from 3.34.0. Adds story briefings for Q001-Q048 without changing quest mechanics or server authority.

- New Game Scenario selector: changing a Quest opens its narrative briefing.
- Added Read Quest Narrative button below the Scenario selector.
- Campaign Downtime: clicking Begin Qxxx opens the narrative first, then Begin Quest confirms the transition.
- Briefing shows World Event/type, full Quest Story, Victory condition, and Reward.
- Escape, Back, or clicking outside closes the briefing.
- Existing Campaign I-IV mechanics and room/server authority remain unchanged.
